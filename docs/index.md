# ScalarScope

Compare two machine-learning runs, and see how sure the comparison is.

## Key Features

- **Compare Two Runs**: two inference traces with B/A at p50, p90 and p99 and their intervals, two training histories, or two ASPIRE geometry exports.
- **Delta Analysis**: each delta says whether it fired, stayed quiet or was withheld, and why.
- **Workbench**: a local model proposes what a setting does; the program tests it and sets the verdict.
- **History**: a project's measures across its logged reviews.
- **Reproducible Bundles**: A matching SHA-256 is a content check, not a signature.
- **Review Mode**: open a saved bundle exactly as saved.
